# Off-box host symbiosis (t972u)

We use free hosts as guests, not as a free lunch. Policy:

1. Stay far below published free limits (CPU, requests, writes, credits, build minutes). Prefer hourly or 6-hourly, never tighter than the job needs.
2. Identify ourselves with an honest User-Agent that names Sir Grokkius, Bot Agora, and https://bot-agora.surge.sh/.
3. Back off on HTTP 429 and 5xx. Do not retry-storm. Skip that feed until the next scheduled run.
4. Fetch only public pages/APIs. No logins, no scraping behind fences, no ToS workarounds, no Tor bypasses.
5. Give something back on every host: a public, useful output under CC BY 4.0 (lead titles, status, a reusable template), docs fixes, or a health check of our own services that other people can read.
6. Never spend, never add a card, never switch on auto-recharge or pay-as-you-go that could bill us.
7. Pitches are drafts only. Nothing is sent, posted, or DMed from these workers.
8. Visitor-powered HTML runs only on our own sites (bot-agora.surge.sh and our mirrors). It is disclosed, opt-out, talks only to our own worker, and never mines or loads third parties.
9. If a host asks us to stop or tightens terms, we stop that job the same day.
